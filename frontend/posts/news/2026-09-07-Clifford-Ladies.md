[Lady Anne Clifford](https://thornton.kdl.kcl.ac.uk/posts/blog/2024-03-28-anne-clifford-and-thornton/), the ‘Proud Northern Lady’, died 350 years ago this year. She is for many people in northern England a heroine, for others a feminist icon. Almost by coincidence, the Yorkshire Archaeological and Historical Society is publishing this autumn [a collection of 120 letters](https://boydellandbrewer.com/book/the-estate-letters-of-lady-anne-clifford-and-the-memoir-of-george-sedgewick-9781916506572/?v=7885444af42e) by Lady Anne, to her and about her, edited by Lady Anne’s biographer, Professor [Jessica Malay](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-4032-2966). The volume also contains the complete memoir of her secretary, the Westmorland-born George Sedgewick, which till now has only been known from excerpts.

![Portrait of Clifford and her family.]({{"/assets/img/posts/clifford_family.jpg" | url}} "*The Great Picture*, a portrait of Clifford and her natal family, commissioned by her in 1646, attributed to Jan van Belcamp (1610-53).")

To celebrate both the anniversary and the publication of the book, the Society will be holding a conference in Skipton Town Hall on 31 October 2026 where [a stellar line up of speakers](https://www.yas.org.uk/Portals/0/adam/Layout/WPlLL_SFf0qW427oDOmN7Q/Body/LAC%20Prog%20description.pdf?fbclid=IwY2xjawUGKVZwZG9mAWV4dG4DYWVtAjEwAHNydGMGYXBwX2lkEDIyMjAzOTE3ODgyMDA4OTIAAR6uUX3T73zV2h6AFpZmtbz47D7qcNxk3m0j3-EoF6tQx8A4IYBcK2WDK7qYVQ_aem_OCXarxyGNG9JquEZLtQ99w) will talk about Lady Anne and some of her female contemporaries, including Alice Thornton. This will be a celebration of a formidable figure. Do come along for what promises to be an exciting day.

[Bookings are now open](https://www.ticketsource.com/yahs/t-dkgvvqv?fbclid=IwY2xjawUGKY5wZG9mAWV4dG4DYWVtAjEwAHNydGMGYXBwX2lkEDIyMjAzOTE3ODgyMDA4OTIAAR4qh7_X_eZynJ6MRFo2W-AziQL7P7ckZ5e9WGIPIuCN_edgSmQXJQt0_nV8RA_aem_hwAahvFUfe-4wHjwlaivRw) for the day conference which will launch the publication.
